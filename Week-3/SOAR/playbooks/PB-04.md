**Source:** Exchange receiver (`SIEM_EXCHANGE_005_*_AI_Triage.json`)
**Tier max:** **Tier 0 only** (ticket + audit; no proposed actions)

## What the evidence proves
- Two `New-MailboxExportRequest` records against `Administrator@attackrange.local`,
  both with `.aspx` destinations under the Exchange OWA auth directory.

## What the evidence does NOT prove
- Export completion, file creation, web-shell deployment, or exfiltration
  (Week-2 Exchange report §6–7).

The Exchange receiver (`SIEM_EXCHANGE_005_AI_Receiver.py`, `analyze_exchange_evidence`)
hard-codes `qa_status: "REVIEW_REQUIRED"` regardless of warnings. It also has no
screenshots in the Week-2 evidence index and its human review is unconfirmed. Automating
any response action on this evidence would be indefensible.

`dispatcher.run_playbook()` enforces the cap in code: for PB-04, `proposed = []`
regardless of what the YAML contains.
