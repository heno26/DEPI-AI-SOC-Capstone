**Alert rule:** `SIEM_AUTH_002_SSH_BRUTE_FORCE`
**Source:** SIEM06 receiver (`SIEM06_Auth_002_AI_Triage_*.json`)
**Tier max:** Tier 1 (propose only)

## What the evidence proves
- 30 failed SSH password attempts from `183.62.140.253` in one source minute (`Dec 10 11:01`),
  targeting `root`.
- Count independently verified against OpenObserve: 30.
- 8 source log lines sampled with SHA-256 hashes.

- Successful login (Week-2 SSH report §5).
- Credential compromise.
- Current live attack.
